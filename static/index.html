Library & Community Resources

Seed Libraries

Many public libraries host seed libraries where you can take free seeds and return seeds from your harvest.

  • Ask your local public library about their seed library program
  • Search online for "[your city] seed library"
  • Community gardens and food banks often share seeds too
  • Learn to save seeds from your own plants

Tool Lending Libraries

Borrow gardening tools instead of buying — save money and storage space.

  • Shovels, rakes, hoes, and hand tools
  • Rototillers and cultivators
  • Wheelbarrows and garden carts
  • Pruning tools and loppers

Growing Equipment

Some libraries lend growing equipment to help you get started.

  • Grow lights and heat mats for seed starting
  • Soil test kits to check your soil health
  • Rain gauges and garden tools
  • Canning equipment for preserving harvest

Knowledge Resources

Learn from experts in your community.

  • Master Gardener programs (free expert advice)
  • Library gardening book collections
  • Community workshops and classes
  • Online courses and video tutorials
